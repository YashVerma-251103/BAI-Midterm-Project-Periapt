### **List of Project Topics**

#### **Regulated Industries**

*High compliance friction, strict safety standards, legal liability, and audit-trail requirements.*

##### **1\. Defence, Space & Cybersecurity**

* **B2B / B2G (Government):**  
  * **Adversarial AI Defence (Computer Vision):** Hardening models against physical/digital spoofing (e.g., camouflaged military assets like tanks).  
  * **Day-Zero Vulnerability Prioritisation (Predictive ML):** Autonomous systems scanning, scoring, and prioritising unpatched security flaws in critical infrastructure.  
  * **Encrypted Edge Communications (AI Protocols):** Protocol-level AI protecting drone swarms, IoT networks, and satellites against jamming.  
  * **Submarine & Fleet Logistics (Predictive / GenAI):** Maintenance documentation, vessel diagnostics, and troop/goods movement optimisation.  
  * **Multi-Lingual Tactical Translation (Voice NLP):** Low-latency voice-to-voice translation engines for tactical operations.  
  * **Spacecraft Mission Operations (Spatial AI / CV):** Systems controlling launch monitoring, orbital manoeuvres, uncrewed landers, and telemetry analytics.  
  * **Suspicious Activity Detection (CV / Video Analytics):** Computer vision engines for real-time border and perimeter surveillance.

##### **2\. Healthcare & Biotech**

* **B2B (Enterprise / Provider / Insurer / Pharma)**  
  * **Unstructured Clinical Document Digitisation (CV / OCR):** Medical handwriting recognition combined with confidential consultation transcription to eliminate manual record-entry bottlenecks, parse unstructured physician charts, and integrate legacy paper files into structured EHR systems.  
  * **AI-Assisted Remote Surgery (Computer Vision / Spatial AI):** VR-guided real-time surgical assist tools for expert intervention, overlaying 3D anatomical projections onto surgical viewports to reduce procedural risks and allow off-site specialists to direct complex operations.  
  * **AI Drug Discovery Pipeline (Generative / Deep Learning):** Molecular modelling to accelerate drug candidate identification and clinical trial matching, utilising graph neural networks to simulate binding affinities and generate novel therapeutic molecules in silico.  
  * **Healthcare AI Governance & Auditability (Explainable AI):** Diagnostic trust engines for evaluating hallucination risks and liability frameworks, using local model explanations (e.g., SHAP/LIME) to audit clinical prediction pipelines and ensure regulatory compliance.  
  * **Automated Prior Authorisation & Claims Intelligence (LLM / Agentic RCM):** Multi-agent LLM systems that parse unstructured patient charts against dynamic insurance payer policies to instantly generate approval-ready prior authorisations and flag unbilled medical coding errors.  
  * **Predictive Pharmacovigilance & Post-Market Surveillance (Generative AI / NLP):** Continuous NLP ingestion pipelines that monitor medical research papers, EHR event logs, and patient registry updates to identify unintended adverse drug reactions and signal safety alerts years faster than manual reporting.

* **B2C (Consumer / Patient / On-Demand Services)**  
  * **Biometric Wearable Health Analytics (Edge AI / DIY):** Wearable signal processing for on-device health monitoring and anomaly detection, using low-power TinyML models to process continuous ECG and oxygen saturation data without streaming raw data to the cloud.  
  * **Urgent Care Virtual Triage (Conversational AI):** Initial symptom checkers provide preliminary patient routing, leveraging medically fine-tuned conversational engines to assess condition severity and guide users to appropriate care settings.  
  * **Mass-Mobility On-Demand Urgent Care (Spatial AI / Logistics Engine):** On-demand mobile urgent care with AI-driven transportable diagnostic units, brought to the patient using ride-share fleet platforms (e.g., Uber).   
  * **Personalised Genomic Interpretation & Multi-Omics Coaching (Generative AI):** Consumer-facing LLM agents that translate complex direct-to-consumer genetic sequencing data into plain-language health action plans, linking personal genomic markers with targeted nutrition, exercise, and preventative care schedules.

##### **3\. Financial Services & FinTech**

* **B2B (Institutional / Enterprise):**  
  * **Cross-Border Fraud Detection (Multi-Modal ML):** Real-time deepfake detection and transaction monitoring for B2B payments.  
  * **Automated Investment Research & Trading (Predictive / Generative):** Algorithmic trading, portfolio risk management, and automated credit underwriting.  
  * **Agentic Commerce & Payment Governance (FinTech):** Verification protocols and fraud prevention for autonomous agents handling payments 

* **B2C (Consumer):**  
  * **AI Wealth Management & Financial Advisory (GenAI / ML):** Robo-advisory engines for personal asset management and automated credit scoring.  
  * **P2P Payment Routing Optimisation (FinTech):** Dynamic routing for consumer transactions across multiple platforms   
  * **Multibagger Stock Prediction (Predictive ML):** AI-driven identification of companies with strong financials, resilient and diversified operations, and distinctive products or business models to identify high-growth investment opportunities early.

##### **4\. Human Resources & Legal**

* **B2B (Enterprise):**  
  * **Trust-Verified Recruitment Platforms (CV / Audio ML):** Interview proctoring, identity verification, and anti-fraud candidate evaluations.  
  * **Legal Research & Contract Analysis (Domain LLMs):** Domain-specific contract analysis, precedent discovery, and virtual legal assistants.

#### **Unregulated & Lightly Regulated Industries**

*Faster market cycles, lower compliance barriers, and a focus on user experience and productivity.*

##### **1\. Enterprise Tech, IT Operations & Software**

* **B2B:**  
  * **Vibe Coding & Automated Software Development (LLMs / GenAI):** Prompt-driven code generation, refactoring, and AI-assisted bug patching.  
  * **GenAI Interactive Technical Manuals (NLP / RAG):** Multilingual RAG systems for industrial equipment documentation.  
  * **Sovereign AI Deployment Infrastructure (MLOps):** Platforms enabling local enterprise hosting of open-source vs. proprietary SLMs and LLMs.  
  * **Threat Detection & Incident Response (SOAR):** Continuous network monitoring tools automating endpoint isolation and threat triage.  
  * **AI-Powered Data Integration (AI / Data Engineering):** AI-assisted integration of diverse data sources to improve data accessibility, consistency, and decision-making for businesses. 

##### **2\. Supply Chain, Logistics & Mobility**

* **B2B:**  
  * **Adverse Weather Perception Systems (Computer Vision):** Computer vision models optimised for severe fog, rain, and low-light commercial transport.  
  * **Predictive Fleet & Asset Logistics (Predictive ML):** Supply chain demand forecasting and real-time route optimisation for freight fleets.  
* **B2C:**  
  * **Autonomous Robotaxi Services (Spatial AI / Autonomous Systems):** Urban autonomous mobility platforms and fleet routing infrastructure.

##### **3\. Marketing, Media & Commerce**

* **B2B:**  
  * **Generative AI Marketing Engine (GenAI / Analytics):** Cross-platform ad generation, strategic campaign planning, and automated allocation.  
  * **Conversational Service Agents (Voice AI):** Multilingual engagement, administrative triage, and customer support bots.  
* **B2C / Media:**  
  * **AI-Powered Pre-Production (Generative Media):** Generative storyboarding, script development, and synthetic video rendering.

##### **4\. Education Tech**

* **B2B (Institutional):**  
  * **Academic Integrity Verification (NLP / Predictive):** Detection engines assessing submission originality versus synthetic generation.  
  * **AI-Assisted Examination & Assessment Automation (Computer Vision / OCR):** AI-powered processing of scanned examination sheets, including answer-sheet alignment, OMR detection, handwriting processing, automated evaluation of high-confidence responses, and human review of ambiguous cases.   
* **B2C (Learners):**  
  * **Personalised AI Tutors (Conversational AI):** Adaptive multilingual learning companions adjusting to student learning pace.

**Overview**

This assignment is an opportunity to conceptualise and design a fictitious company where Artificial Intelligence is a core component. 

Your primary task is to develop a **story of this company**: *What problem is this company solving? How does AI create value? Why could this company succeed? Who is it serving, and who are the stakeholders? How will it manage the risks that come with using AI?* 

The five major areas that you should address to build a coherent story about your fictitious company are listed below. Do not treat them as independent components, but use them together to build a compelling story.

**A. Core Components**

**Overview of your company** (*What is your company narrative & vision?*) 

* **Brand Identity:** Introduce your company name, mission, and long-term vision.  
* **Market Opportunity:** Define the targeted industry sector and why it is ripe for AI modernisation.

1. **Agentic AI & Value Proposition** (*What is AI actually doing, and why does it matter?*)  
   * **Autonomous Core:**   
     1. What problem or workflow is the company addressing?  
     2. What role does AI play in solving it?  
     3. Where could agentic AI execute or assist with a multi-step workflow?  
   * **Economic Value:**   
     1. What value does the company create for the customer? 

     You can detail the distinct operational efficiencies, cost reductions, or speed advantages delivered to buyers.

2. **Moat & Defensibility** (*If this is a good idea, then what makes the company difficult to copy or replace?*)  
   * **Core Product Architecture:** Outline the flagship platform's key features and technical capabilities.  
   * **Competitive Moat:** Define your long-term defensibility—such as proprietary data flywheels, unique integrations, or high switching costs.

3. **Porter’s Five Forces** (*What does the competitive environment look like for this business?*)  
   * **Industry Dynamics:**   
     1. Buyer power,  
     2. Supplier power,   
     3. Competitive rivalry,   
     4. Threat of substitutes,   
     5. Threat of new entrants.

     The framework should help you make a strategic point about your company rather than become a standalone textbook analysis. 

4. **Persona & Customer Journey** (*Who is the customer, what problem do they have, and what happens when they use your solution?*)  
   * **Target Persona:** Profile the ideal buyer, detailing their key pain points, goals, and decision-making criteria.  
   * **End-to-End Journey:** Map the customer experience from initial onboarding through agentic automation to full value realisation and renewal.

5. **Governance, Guardrails & Regional Compliance** (*What happens when the AI gets something wrong?*)  
   * **Trust & Safety:**   
     1. Where is human oversight needed,  
     2. Human-in-the-loop (HITL) overrides,  
     3. When should the AI stop or escalate,  
     4. Hallucination and reliability safeguards,  
     5. Monitoring and safety audits,  
     6. Data privacy and relevant regulation.

   * **Jurisdictional Compliance:** You may choose one from the following to highlight the regulatory adherence tailored to your target market.  
     1. **United States:** NIST AI Risk Management Framework, CCPA/CPRA data privacy, FTC algorithmic fairness, and sector-specific rules (HIPAA/FCRA).  
     2. **United Kingdom:** UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, ICO guidance, Article 22 automated decision rights, and AISI safety benchmarks.  
     3. **India:** Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, Consent Manager frameworks, IT Rules for automated intermediaries, and data localisation.

These five areas should connect with each other. The points listed in each component are for your assistance and not a mandatory checklist.

**F. Academic & Submission Guidelines**

Please adhere strictly to the following academic standards.

* **Company Originality:** You must create a new, fictitious company of any size. Submissions may not use an existing company.

* **Plagiarism**: Submissions must show less than 10% similarity, excluding references, quotes, and common phrases.

* **Use of Generative AI Tools:**

  * **Permitted Uses:** You may use GenAI tools (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, or other AI tools) to assist you with brainstorming, exploring ideas and alternatives, structuring your thinking, research assistance, critiquing or stress-testing ideas, refining communication or presentation. It should be an **assistant to your thinking, not a substitute for it**.

  * **Prohibited Uses:** It is prohibited to submit AI-generated answers without revision, copy responses verbatim, or use AI to bypass critical thinking and originality. **Blindly copying or submitting AI-generated material as your own work is not permitted.** 

* **References:** You must include 3-4 key references that are important to support your research. Please note that blogs are not considered key references.

**G. Review & Viva Process**

To maintain academic integrity, submissions may go through a three-tier Human-in-the-Loop review:

* Tier 1 — TA Review: The assigned TA evaluates the submission or flags it as questionable if concerns arise around originality, understanding, or AI use.  
* Tier 2 — TA Discussion: Questionable submissions are reviewed by a second TA and, where necessary, discussed collectively by the TAs.  
* Tier 3 — Oral Viva: Submissions that remain unresolved may be referred for a short viva with the instructor and TAs, during which students may be asked to explain and defend their work.

**Note:** *The viva is not intended to penalise the use of AI; it is to verify that students understand and can defend their submission.*
